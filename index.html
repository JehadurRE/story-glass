How it works

Three steps. That’s it.

01

Copy the link

Open the story or video → Share → Copy link. Full URL, not just a username.

02

Paste & view

Drop it in the box above. We resolve media and open a story-style player.

03

Watch or save

Step through segments with the rail. Save only what you own or may keep.

Good to know

  • No StoryGlass account — ever
  • No app install required
  • Private or expired content usually won’t load
  • Not a way around login walls or audience limits

FAQ

Do I need to sign up?
No. Paste a public link and watch.
Which links work best?
Facebook videos, page video tabs, Instagram story links with a media id.
Why did it fail?
Story may be private, deleted, or older than ~24 hours. Try the full share link again.
Mobile?
Yes — any modern phone browser.

Having trouble?

If auto-load fails, open the media page in another tab, copy page source or JSON, and paste it below. We only parse — no login needed.
