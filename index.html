Why this exists

The story viewer list is awkward on purpose

Instagram and Facebook made watching public stories feel like signing a guest book. One slip and your name is sitting in their viewer list — often at the worst time.

“Why did they view my story?”

You opened a public story by accident, from a shared phone, or while half-asleep. Twenty-four hours later it’s still a screenshot waiting to happen. Public shouldn’t mean you’re on a list.

Stalking checks that aren’t stalking

Checking a competitor, an ex’s public brunch recap, or a page you’d rather not “engage” with is normal curiosity. Platforms treat every open like a notification you meant to send.

Blocks after one quiet look

People watch viewer lists like hawk. One view from a burner still looks suspicious if it’s the only activity. Getting blocked for existing in a public story’s analytics is the modern “seen zone.”

No “just looking” mode

Feed posts can be scrolled quietly. Stories can’t — the app is designed to surface your identity back to the creator. That’s product choice, not a law of nature.

Good to know

  • No app install required

FAQ

Will my name show up in their story viewer list?
That’s the whole point people look for tools like this. When StoryGlass loads a Facebook public video from page HTML, it does not use your Facebook account — so you don’t appear as a viewer from yourself. Instagram is stricter: story media often needs a server session, and that session (not your face, but still an account) can show up. We don’t sell a magic “invisible” guarantee.
Do I need to sign up?
No. Paste a public link and watch. No StoryGlass login, no Instagram login on your side.
Which links work best?
Facebook videos, page video tabs, Instagram story links with a media id.
Why did it fail / why did I get blocked?
Story may be private, deleted, or older than ~24 hours. If you viewed in the official app and got blocked, that’s the platform’s viewer list doing its job — a browser tool can’t un-send that view.
Is this for stalking?
No. It’s for watching content that’s already public without putting your name on a guest list. Private accounts stay private. Don’t use it to harass anyone.
Mobile?
Yes — any modern phone browser.

Having trouble?

If auto-load fails, open the media page in another tab, copy page source or JSON, and paste it below. We only parse — no login needed.
