How to use

01
Copy the link In Instagram or Facebook, open the story or video → Share → Copy link.
02
Paste and view Drop it in the box above and hit View story.
03
Watch or save Use the rail to step through segments. Save only content you own or may keep.

What you should know

You do not need a StoryGlass account. We do not store your media on our servers for playback — files stream from the platform CDNs when available.

Private or expired stories usually cannot be loaded. This is not a way around login walls or audience limits. Instagram may still count views toward the creator depending on how the content is fetched.

FAQ

Do I need to sign up?

No. Paste a public link and watch.

Which links work best?

Facebook public videos (/watch/?v=, /videos/) and Instagram story links that include a media id. Profile-only links are harder.

Why did it fail?

The story may be private, deleted, or past ~24 hours. Copy the full link again (not just a username). You can also open Having trouble? and paste page source.

Does it work on iPhone / Android?

Yes — any modern mobile browser.

Having trouble?

If auto-load fails, open the media or story page in another tab, copy the page source or JSON, and paste it below. We only parse — we do not need your login.
